About Clawlege

Teaching AI agent development to everyone, for free.

We believe that building your own AI assistant shouldn't require a computer science degree or a paid course.

Our Mission

AI agents are the future of personal computing. Yet most resources assume you already know how to code, charge hundreds of dollars, or both. Clawlege exists to change that.

Free and Open Source, Forever

Every module, every template, every line of code is available on GitHub under the MIT license. No signup required, no credit card, no "upgrade to premium" — just learning.

Built on OpenClaw

OpenClaw is an open source AI agent framework that makes building personal assistants accessible to everyone. Clawlege teaches you how to harness its power, starting from zero experience.

For Everyone

Whether you're 12 or 72, whether you've never written code or you ship software for a living, Clawlege meets you where you are. Our progressive curriculum starts with plain English and gradually introduces more advanced concepts.

Real Agents, Real Value

This isn't theory or toy examples. By the end of Module 4, you'll have a personal AI assistant that remembers your preferences, searches the web, and helps with daily tasks. By Module 9, you'll have shipped something the world can use.

The Team

Created by the OpenClaw community of developers, educators, and AI enthusiasts.

Clawlege was born from countless hours of community discussions, feedback from new users, and a shared belief that AI education should be accessible to everyone.

Want to contribute? Check out our GitHub repository or join the conversation on Discord.
